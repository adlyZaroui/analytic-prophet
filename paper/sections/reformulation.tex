\section{An exact reformulation}
\label{sec:reformulation}

Write each rate as the difference of two non-negative parts,
$\delta_j = \delta_j^+ - \delta_j^-$ with $\delta_j^+, \delta_j^- \ge 0$, and
replace $\lvert\delta_j\rvert$ in \eqref{eq:objective} by $\delta_j^+ + \delta_j^-$.
The penalty becomes linear, so the objective is smooth, and the non-smoothness
moves into simple bound constraints that L-BFGS-B handles natively. The
reformulation is standard for $\ell_1$ problems; what needs stating is that it is
exact here, and why.

\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:exact}
Let $F(\delta)$ be any function of $\delta$ alone, and $\tau > 0$. If
$(\delta^{+\star}, \delta^{-\star})$ minimises
$F(\delta^+ - \delta^-) + \tau^{-1}\sum_j(\delta_j^+ + \delta_j^-)$ over
$\delta^+, \delta^- \ge 0$, then $\delta^{+\star}_j\,\delta^{-\star}_j = 0$ for
every $j$, and $\delta^\star = \delta^{+\star} - \delta^{-\star}$ minimises
$F(\delta) + \tau^{-1}\lVert\delta\rVert_1$, with the same optimal value.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Suppose $c = \min(\delta^{+\star}_j, \delta^{-\star}_j) > 0$ for some $j$.
Subtracting $c$ from both leaves $\delta^\star_j$, and therefore $F$, unchanged
while reducing the penalty by $2c/\tau > 0$, contradicting optimality. So every
pair has a zero member, and then $\delta^{+\star}_j + \delta^{-\star}_j =
\lvert\delta^\star_j\rvert$. For any feasible point,
$\delta_j^+ + \delta_j^- \ge \lvert\delta_j^+ - \delta_j^-\rvert$, so the split
objective is bounded below by the $\ell_1$ objective at the corresponding
$\delta$, with equality on the complementary pairs. Every $\ell_1$ point is
reached by such a pair, so the two problems share their optimal value and
$\delta^\star$ attains it.
\end{proof}

The proposition makes no use of convexity and holds for any likelihood, so it
covers Prophet's multiplicative and logistic configurations too, where several
other arguments in this paper do not apply.

\begin{remark}
\label{rem:exponential}
If $\delta^+_j, \delta^-_j$ are independent exponential variables with rate
$1/\tau$, then $\delta^+_j - \delta^-_j \sim \mathrm{Laplace}(0, \tau)$, and the
split objective is exactly the negative log of the \emph{joint} posterior over
$(\delta^+, \delta^-)$ under those priors. The reformulation therefore has a
probabilistic reading as well as an algebraic one: it preserves Prophet's prior
as a marginal.

That reading is reassuring, but it is \emph{not} why the MAP estimate is
unchanged. The mode of a joint density is not in general the mode of its
marginal, so preserving the prior as a marginal would not by itself imply that
the two problems share a minimiser. They do here because of
\cref{prop:exact} --- complementarity --- and not because of the distributional
identity.
\end{remark}
